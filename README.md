# 🏠 Gather Haus

**A creative community platform built for collaboration, storytelling, and soul.**  
This space is for local creators—artists, healers, builders, and dreamers—to link up, share their work, and co-create magic together.

---

## ✨ What It Is

Gather Haus was born from the question: *“How can I find other creators like me?”*

As a parent, a creator, and a community lover, I needed a digital home that could help me—and others—capture stories, find collaborators, and grow our networks with purpose.  
This app is our answer: a soulful, expressive space where creativity meets connection.

---

## 🔧 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** EJS, TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express
- **Database:** MongoDB + Mongoose
- **Auth:** bcrypt, express-session
- **Image Uploads:** Cloudinary
- **Extras:** Multer, Method-Override, Flash Messages
- **Design Tools:** Figma

---

## 🧩 Features

- 🔐 **Authentication** (login/signup for creators and organizers)
- 🧑🏽‍🎨 **Creator Onboarding & Dashboard**  
  Set your bio, craft, location, collab status, and upload a profile image.
- 📸 **Image Upload Gallery**  
  Upload your work and build your visual portfolio (Cloudinary integrated).
- 🗓️ **Event Creation & Hosting**  
  Organizers can create events and invite local creators to join.
- 🤝 **Collab Board**  
  Post and browse collab opportunities filtered by type (photoshoot, event, project, etc).
- 🧠 **AI Features (Coming Soon)**  
  - Smart bio generator  
  - Event matchmaking based on location and craft  
  - Pre-written message templates for outreach

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Gather Haus uses a bold yet minimal aesthetic to mirror the soul of the community it supports. Brand colors pull from our logo:

- `#7E5DFA` – electric purple  
- `#1F1F1F` – deep charcoal  
- `#C1C1C1` – soft gray  
- `#FFFFFF` – clean white  
- `#000000` – pure black
